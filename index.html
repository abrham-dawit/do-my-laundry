Want to be a washer?

The other half of the idea: people with a car and a washing machine (or a laundromat they like) earn money picking up and doing laundry for people near them. In the real app, washers go through an identity check before their first pickup — that's what makes it safe for both sides.

Honest note: washer sign-up isn't wired up in this demo, and neither are payments or real pickup dispatch. Orders here are saved only in your browser, and the status buttons on each order simulate the flow so you can click through the whole experience. See the README.
